Videos and songs on the server

We're asking everyone again since this now covers songs as well. Your previous answers are already filled in below.

Players can now upload their own songs and recordings, and they turn up in game as cassettes, CDs and vinyl you can play in any music player. The songs come through the launcher, the same way the cinema videos do.

Some of the videos and songs are going to be copyrighted, just like the songs from Lifestyle and the server's music mods are. If you don't want any copyrighted stuff sent to you, you can opt out. You'll still get everything that isn't copyrighted, and copyrighted songs just play silent for you.

The videos are heavily compressed, so they use very little disk space, and we'll archive older ones if they start piling up. The launcher can also download new videos while your game is running. If you opt out of that, you'll need to restart the game to get any added videos.
